## Goal and acceptance criteria

Make storyboard review the required stopping point for prompt/video generation:
the orchestrator creates the brief, story, script/plan, and complete beat
storyboard panels, then waits for an explicit user continuation before
keyframes, clips, assembly, and export. The Storyboard page must clearly
explain the review state and offer one accessible continuation action.

## Decisions

- The server, not a client checkbox or `runThrough` flag, owns the
  post-storyboard stop.
- Treat continuation or change requests as approval-gate actions, not direct
  content mutations.
- Required scene-level visual anchors are deferred: the current scene wireframe
  is optional/disposable and needs its own asset/tool/conditioning contract.

## Changes

- Entry-point runs create only an `after:generate_storyboard` gate; legacy
  `reviewGates`, `stopAfter`, and `runThrough` cannot stop before the board.
- The board offers **Generate video**; approval reopens the completed planning
  pass and continues production.
- Post-storyboard review projection, rejection behavior, dashboard activity,
  and produced-stage identity are maintained as part of the PR feedback fixes.
