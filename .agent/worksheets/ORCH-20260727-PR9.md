## Decisions

- Kept cost/credit provenance in `model_call_costs` and `credit_transactions`.
  The new reservation table controls admission/settlement only; it does not
  duplicate a charge ledger.
- Budget control serializes through the root/direct family and keys operations
  to durable action/job identities. A direct confirmation reserves the finite
  run ceiling, so later operation reservations do not double-reserve it.
- A completed provider job settles its deterministic accepted estimate;
  failures release the reservation. The recovery sweep repairs terminal claims,
  terminal-job accounting, parent wakes, and unacknowledged domain waits after
  a worker crash.
- Async platform-key generation settles its balance-guarded credit debit in the
  same idempotent reservation transaction; accepted jobs therefore do not rely
  on the synchronous engine debit branch.
- Recovery reuses billing attribution persisted on the active reservation
  before a provider job becomes terminal, and atomically projects an
  unacknowledged child report onto its parent delegation before waking it.
- A creator-direct run ceiling is family-wide across continuation successors;
  later operations consume remaining approved capacity without reserving a
  second ceiling.
