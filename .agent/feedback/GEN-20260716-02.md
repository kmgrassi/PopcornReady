## Lesson

Stopping after a completed storyboard cannot be modeled as a generic terminal
success: the run projection must expose an actionable review state, and
approval must reopen the pass before dispatching production work. Keeping that
policy at the entry-point route prevents old client controls such as
`runThrough` from quietly bypassing the creator's review boundary.

## Follow-up

Implement scene-level visual anchors as first-class generated assets, require
them before beat storyboard generation, and pass the selected anchors as real
provider references. The current scene wireframe remains an optional,
disposable review aid.
